```
Your Platform Capability

✅ FULLY SUPPORTED:
   • Workshops (single price, quantity limit)
   • Meetups (free or single-price ticketed)
   • Webinars (free, registration-based)
   • Simple festivals (multiple ticket types at fixed prices)
   • Basic conferences (GA + VIP, fixed pricing)

⚠️ PARTIALLY SUPPORTED:
   • Multi-day festivals (can make multiple ticket types, but not bundles)
   • Conferences with early bird (works but UI limited)
   • Theater (infrastructure exists, not connected)
   • Concert (works for simple single-price)

❌ NOT SUPPORTED (yet):
   • Theater with assigned seating (no seat→price linking)
   • Premium conferences (no add-ons: parking, meals, VIP lounge)
   • Sports events (no section-based pricing)
   • Resale/secondary market (no transfer system)
   • Complex venue pricing (no venue→price rules)
   • Festival with group discounts
   • Concert with tiered seating (orchestra, mezzanine, balcony)
   • Charity galas (no auction/donation tiers)
```

### What Eventbrite supports

```
✅ FULLY SUPPORTED:
   • All of the above (100%+)
   • Theater with 50+ pricing tiers (per section)
   • Complex tiered concerts (VIP meets + floor access)
   • Sports with dynamic pricing (demand-based)
   • Festivals with group discounts + bundles
   • Resale marketplace (attend, then resell on secondary market)
   • Corporate events (block ticketing, role-based pricing)
   • Hybrid events (in-person + online at different prices)
```
