# IMPORTANT INFORMATION

1. For E-Commerce or Online Businesses: Provide Company Profile and Active Online Link.  
2. All Energy and Petroleum Businesses must provide EPRA License and/or Contract from Contracting Dealership.  
3. If more than two persons are listed as business owners on the Business Permit/Certificate of Registration, BOR must be filled and signed by all partners and include Identification Documents and KRA Pins.  
4. If trading under a different name than on the Certificate of Registration, provide Trade Name Certificate.  
5. Ensure Bank Name, Account Name, and Account Number are accurately captured.  
6. For Tour Operators: Physical Visit Summary must include number of employees, office status (shared or not), merchant’s daily involvement, vehicle ownership, and client sourcing.  
7. If an Estate is a shareholder: Provide Grant of Probate and Letter on Letterhead from Authorized Signatories with ID copy.  
8. Updated CR12 and new Directors’ Identification & KRA Pins required upon change of ownership.  
9. All exemption requests must be escalated to the Risk Team via email before uploading to CRM.  
10. Submit clear, legible documents with all edges visible and not cropped.  
11. Airlines: Registration with recognized Airlines Body (e.g., KCAA) and Physical Visit required.  
12. Travel and Tour Operators: Travel and Tour Operator License required.  
13. Mandatory for KE Travel & Tour Accounts:
   - Membership of TRA  
   - Membership in KATO or KATA

# ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR TILL REQUEST

1. Duly Filled and Signed Application Form & Terms and Conditions  
2. Filled, Stamped or Sealed M-PESA Authorization Form signed by at least two (2) Directors or Shareholders  
3. Copy of CR12 (Validity: 90 Days) or Recent Annual Returns with Filing Receipt  
4. If Publicly Listed Company is a Shareholder: Company Secretary Letter listing Majority Shareholders/Directors (Full Name, Nationality, DOB, Document Type & Number)  
5. If more than 5 Directors/Shareholders: Collect IDs & KRA Pins for Top 5 Majority Shareholders + Letter from Company Secretary listing others  
6. If Majority Shareholders are Companies:
   - CR12 for those companies  
   - Identification Documents for Directors & Shareholders  
   - KRA Pin Certificates (Kenyan Companies) or Compliance Certificates (Foreign Companies)  
7. For Additional Tills (LLCs): Existing CR12 must be less than 2 years old; otherwise provide updated CR12 (within 3 months).  
8. If an Estate is a Shareholder: Provide Grant of Probate and Authorized Signatory ID.  
9. All Till Requests must be routed through Sales Ops.
